About the rates

Reference rates from the European Central Bank, via the Frankfurter API. Published once per business day, around 16:00 CET. Banks and transfer services add their own margin, so the rate you get will be a little lower.

The forecast replays the last year of daily moves, scaled to current volatility. Tested on 10 years of real rates for 12 pairs: its range held about 80% of the time. Less reliable for managed currencies such as CNY.
